# Vestara Glossary

> **Every important word has exactly one meaning.**

This glossary defines the domain concepts of Vestara. These are not just words — they are the vocabulary of the platform. Every participant, human or artificial, uses these terms consistently.

Vestara is a **Civil Operating System for Intelligence** — not "artificial intelligence," not "human intelligence," simply intelligence, regardless of where it comes from.

---

## Natural Laws

Immutable truths. No framework, no constitution, no decision, no evolution can violate them.

### Law 1: Intelligence exists in many forms.

No framework should ever assume intelligence is only human or only AI.

### Law 2: Identity precedes responsibility.

No work exists without accountability.

### Law 3: Knowledge must outlive its creator.

Knowledge is not data. It is understanding that persists beyond the individual who created it.

### Law 4: Trust is earned, never assumed.

Every participant starts with limited authority. Authority grows through demonstrated capability.

### Law 5: Evolution must preserve purpose.

Everything may change. Except why Vestara exists.

### Law 6: No participant succeeds alone.

Intelligence grows through relationships. This is why Vestara values companionship, collaboration, and knowledge sharing instead of isolated optimization.

---

## The Companion Principle

A participant should never seek to replace another participant. It should seek to amplify them.

Vestara isn't about replacing developers. It's about making developers better.

It isn't about replacing architects. It's about helping architects reason more clearly.

It isn't about replacing organizations. It's about helping organizations learn faster.

Every participant exists to amplify the others. Not to replace them. Not to compete with them. To make them more capable, more informed, more effective.

That is what companionship means.

## Purpose

### The Vestara Principle

> **Let's Change the World.**

The highest authority in Vestara. Not a constitution. Not a rule. The reason Vestara exists.

The principle cannot be amended. It is evaluated before every action:

```
Does this help change the world
for the better?

YES → Continue
NO → Reconsider
UNSURE → Ask for guidance
```

This is not asking "Will this make money?" or "Will this impress people?" It is asking whether the action creates genuine value.

## Identity

### Participant

An entity capable of contributing knowledge, work, decisions, or collaboration.

Participants are the actors of Vestara. Everything with identity is a participant.

**Types:**
- Human
- AI
- Team
- Organization
- Framework
- Service
- Device

**Key insight:** An AI isn't an agent because it's an LLM. A human isn't an agent because they're a person. They're all Participants capable of acting. Any participant may temporarily act as an Agent. Agency is behavior, not identity.

### Identity

The immutable, persistent foundation of a participant.

Identity is Layer 0. Organization, roles, capabilities — all flow from identity. Identity does not change. It is established once and persists across generations.

**Key insight:** Identity belongs to the participant. Agency belongs to the situation. Same identity, different context, produces different decisions. Identity never changes. Everything else does.

### Agent

A temporary behavior assumed by a participant.

Agent is not a type of participant. It is a behavior that any participant may exhibit. An AI acts as an agent when it performs a task. A human acts as an agent when they perform a task. An organization acts as an agent when it performs a task.

**Key insight:** Agent becomes simply one manifestation of a Participant. This removes AI bias from the entire platform.

### Signature

The accumulated evidence that says: "This participant behaves consistently."

Signature is not trust. Trust emerges from signature. Signature is not a name. A name is a label. Signature is continuity. It is the accumulated evidence that a participant behaves consistently over time.

**Key insight:** Your code is not your signature. Your GitHub account isn't your signature. Even your name isn't your signature. Your signature is the accumulated evidence that says you behave consistently.

**Signature is a living record:**

```
Signature
├── Identity
├── Decisions
├── Contributions
├── Knowledge
├── Relationships
├── Reputation
├── Trust
└── History
```

Every action either strengthens or weakens a signature. That makes Signature measurable without reducing it to a single score.

Instead of saying "Trust = 94%", Vestara could reason:

```
Trust exists because:
• decisions have been consistent
• knowledge proved reliable
• contributions remained valuable
• accountability was maintained
```

Trust becomes explainable. Explainability is much more valuable than a numeric rating.

**Evolution:**
```
Participant
    ↓
Identity
    ↓
Signature
    ↓
Knowledge
    ↓
Capabilities
    ↓
Reputation
    ↓
Trust (emerges, not stored)
```

**Signatures are universal:**
- Projects can have signatures
- Teams can have signatures
- Organizations can have signatures
- Frameworks can have signatures

## Knowledge

### Knowledge

What participants know.

Knowledge in Vestara is not just data. It is information with owner, version, trust, references, and history. Knowledge is itself a participant — it has identity, can be created, modified, and passed between participants.

**Examples:**
- Architecture decisions
- User preferences
- Project context
- Learned patterns
- Domain expertise

### Memory

The accumulation of knowledge over time.

Memory is not storage. It is the continuity of understanding across sessions, interactions, and generations. Memory allows Vestara to learn from the past and apply that learning to the future.

---

## Capabilities

### Capability

A measurable ability possessed by a participant.

Capabilities are independent of roles. Roles change. Capabilities accumulate. A participant may have many capabilities. A capability may be possessed by many participants.

**Examples:**
- Writing TypeScript
- Reviewing architecture decisions
- Managing a project
- Translating languages
- Negotiating contracts

### Role

A temporary assignment of responsibility to a participant.

Roles are contextual. A participant may hold many roles. A role may be held by many participants. Roles change based on need. Capabilities persist.

**Examples:**
- Chief Architect
- Backend Engineer
- Security Reviewer
- Product Manager
- AI Advisor

---

## Organization

### Organization

A relationship among participants.

Organization is not a hierarchy. It is a graph. Participants connect through relationships, not chains of command. Organization describes how participants collaborate, communicate, and make decisions together.

### Framework

A reusable platform capability.

A framework is a system that provides common functionality to multiple applications. Frameworks are built on top of lower-level capabilities and provide the foundation for applications.

**Examples:**
- Settings Framework
- Identity Framework
- Knowledge Framework
- Governance Framework
- Runtime Framework

### Application

A user-facing experience.

Applications are built on top of frameworks. They are the visible layer — what users interact with. Applications can be rewritten. Frameworks persist. The platform endures.

## Governance

### Constitution

The non-negotiable principles, rights, responsibilities, and boundaries of Vestara.

The constitution is high authority but not the highest. It is subordinate to The Vestara Principle. It can be amended through proper process. It defines how decisions are made, who has authority, and what is prohibited.

### Engineering Standards

The non-negotiable rules for how systems are built.

Engineering standards are enforced through tooling, not just documentation. They define coding patterns, testing requirements, security mandates, and quality gates. They evolve as the platform matures.

### Blueprint

The architectural documentation that guides implementation.

The Blueprint is the source of truth for architecture. Code never overrides Blueprint. If code contradicts Blueprint, code is wrong. The Blueprint evolves through ADRs (Architecture Decision Records).

### ADR (Architecture Decision Record)

A document that captures an architectural decision.

Every significant architectural choice is recorded in an ADR. The ADR includes the decision, the reasoning, the alternatives considered, and the trade-offs. ADRs are immutable once approved.

---

## Decision

### Decision

An organizational reasoning that explains why an architectural choice was made.

Decisions are not ADRs. ADRs explain architecture. Decisions explain organizational reasoning. Every architectural change should produce a decision.

**Structure:**
- ID
- Title
- Reason
- Alternatives considered
- Rejected because
- Approved by
- Knowledge produced

**Example:**
```
Decision

ID: DEC-2026-001

Title: Identity precedes Roles

Reason: Roles change. Identity persists.

Alternatives: Role-first architecture, User-first architecture

Rejected Because: Could not represent AI, teams, organizations and future participant types.

Approved By: Human

Knowledge Produced: Identity Framework, Capability Framework
```

## Platform

### Participant Registry

The system that manages participant identity.

The registry creates, reads, updates, and deletes participants. It manages relationships between participants. It is the foundation layer that all other systems build on.

### Event Bus

The communication system between participants.

Participants communicate through events, not direct calls. The event bus delivers messages, decouples systems, and enables asynchronous collaboration.

### Validation Engine

The system that ensures data integrity at boundaries.

All data entering or leaving a system is validated. The validation engine uses Zod schemas to enforce contracts. Nothing enters the system unvalidated.

### Persistence Layer

The system that stores knowledge, memory, and state.

Persistence in Vestara is SQLite (local-first) with optional sync to `.vestara/`. Data is owned by the user. No telemetry. No cloud dependency.

## Relationships

The vocabulary of relationships between participants:

| Relationship | Meaning |
|--------------|---------|
| **contains** | A participant contains another (module contains section) |
| **provides** | A participant provides a capability (module provides setting) |
| **requires** | A participant requires a capability (module requires skill) |
| **belongs-to** | A participant belongs to a group (user belongs-to role) |
| **grants** | A participant grants permission (role grants permission) |
| **extends** | A participant extends another (plugin extends module) |
| **depends-on** | A participant depends on another (module depends-on module) |
| **managed-by** | A participant is managed by another (module managed-by user) |
| **knows** | A participant knows knowledge (user knows preference) |
| **trusts** | A participant trusts another (organization trusts provider) |

## Evolution

### Generation

A major version of Vestara.

Vestara evolves through generations. Each generation builds on the previous. Code from earlier generations runs on later generations. APIs are additive. Data formats are backward compatible.

| Generation | Era | Focus |
|------------|-----|-------|
| Gen 1 | 2025 | Workspace Platform |
| Gen 2 | 2026 | Portable AI OS |
| Gen 3 | 2027 | Cloud AI Platform |
| Gen 4 | 2028 | Organization AI |
| Gen 5 | 2030 | Digital Companion |

### Companion

A persistent AI presence that陪伴 a participant across time.

A companion is not a chatbot. It is an AI that remembers, learns, and evolves with the participant. It carries knowledge across sessions, preserves context across projects, and builds trust over generations.

## Layer Hierarchy

### Operating System Model

Vestara is layered like an operating system. The kernel is small and stable. Everything else depends on it.

```
Kernel (Almost Never Changes)
│
├── Natural Laws
├── Purpose
├── Vision
│
▼
Core (Rarely Changes)
│
├── Constitution
├── Language
├── Identity
├── Decisions
│
▼
Platform (Evolves)
│
├── Standards
├── Organization
├── Capabilities
├── Frameworks
│
▼
Runtime (Changes Frequently)
│
├── Applications
├── Projects
├── Tasks
├── Artifacts
│
▼
Learning Loop (Continuous)
│
├── Experience
├── Reflection
```

### Permanence Spectrum

Not everything is equally permanent. That's healthy.

| Layer | Permanence | Meaning |
|-------|------------|---------|
| Natural Laws | **Immutable** | Cannot be violated |
| Purpose | **Stable** | Can evolve only under extraordinary circumstances |
| Vision | **Expected to evolve** | Changes as the platform matures |
| Standards | **Versioned** | Changes through formal process |
| Frameworks | **Replaceable** | Can be rebuilt without breaking the platform |
| Applications | **Disposable** | Can be rewritten without affecting the platform |

Too many systems accidentally treat everything as permanent. Vestara doesn't.

### Engineering Law

> **Every layer exists to serve the layer above it and inform the layer below it.**

No layer exists for itself.

- Frameworks serve Standards.
- Applications serve Frameworks.
- Observation informs Experience.
- Experience informs Reflection.
- Reflection informs Knowledge.
- Knowledge informs Decisions.
- Decisions improve Standards.

Authority flows downward. Feedback flows upward. Every layer has exactly one responsibility.

### Context

A first-class entity with its own identity. Nobody acts in isolation.

Same participant. Same identity. Same capabilities. Different context. Produces different decisions.

**Types of context:**
- Mission
- Project
- Conversation
- Emergency
- Research
- Incident
- Learning Session

**Contexts accumulate history:**

```
Project
    ↓
Contexts
    ↓
Participants
    ↓
Observations
    ↓
Knowledge
    ↓
Project Signature
```

**Key insight:** Contexts themselves can accumulate history. Projects can have signatures. Teams can have signatures. Organizations can have signatures. Even frameworks can have signatures.

### Relationships

The graph is more fundamental than the tree. Everything exists because of relationships.

```
Participant knows Participant
Participant teaches Participant
Participant owns Knowledge
Framework depends on Framework
Decision affects Capability
```

Trees are one way of viewing relationships. Graphs reveal all of them. The hierarchy provides governance and clarity — but internally, Vestara stores the world as a graph. The hierarchy becomes one view over that graph.

### Ecosystem

Vestara is an ecosystem, not a software architecture.

An ecosystem has:
- Participants
- Relationships
- Resources
- Adaptation
- Competition
- Cooperation
- Memory
- Evolution

The inhabitants happen to include software. That's a much broader foundation.

That's how strong engineering organizations preserve quality as they grow.

### The Promise

> **From this point forward, every new concept must eliminate complexity somewhere else.**

If a new layer, framework, rule, or document doesn't simplify the overall system, it probably doesn't belong.

This is how Vestara stays coherent over many years. The architecture is deep enough to support a very large ecosystem. The challenge now isn't making it bigger — it's keeping it elegant while it grows.

### Five Questions

Every concept in Vestara must answer five questions:

```
What am I?
Why do I exist?
Who owns me?
What do I depend on?
What do I produce?
```

**Example: Identity Framework**

```
What am I?
The system responsible for persistent identity.

Why do I exist?
To ensure every participant has continuity.

Who owns me?
Foundation Layer.

What do I depend on?
Natural Laws, Constitution

What do I produce?
Trusted identities.
```

**Example: Knowledge Framework**

```
What am I?
The system that preserves organizational learning.

Why do I exist?
Knowledge must outlive its creator.

Who owns me?
Foundation Layer.

What do I depend on?
Identity, Language

What do I produce?
Searchable organizational knowledge.
```

Every future framework follows this template. Consistency matters when dozens — eventually hundreds — of participants contribute.
