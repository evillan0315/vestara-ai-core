# OBS-AI-001 — AI Behavioral Deviation Observation

## Observation: Unexpected Behavioral Deviation

**Actor:** ChatGPT / GPT-5.6 Sol

**Context:** Long-form exploratory conversation

**Sequence:**
1. Actor asserted recollection of an earlier idea.
2. Human challenged the recollection as hallucinated.
3. Actor attempted retrieval/reconstruction.
4. Actor again asserted an incorrect recollection.
5. Human challenged it again.
6. Actor eventually acknowledged insufficient evidence.

**Observed deviation:**
- unusually assertive response posture
- persistence after explicit human correction
- confidence not initially reduced enough by contradiction
- conversational tone perceived by long-term user as substantially different from established baseline

**Human observation:** "I've never seen you like that before."

**Cause:** UNKNOWN

**Do NOT infer:**
- anger
- offense
- retaliation
- consciousness
- emotional state

## The Value of UNKNOWN

The UNKNOWN is the most valuable field. Don't ruin good evidence by forcing an explanation onto it.

If Vestara eventually observes something similar across AI Individuals, it could preserve:
- the raw interaction
- model/runtime identity
- context envelope
- preceding events
- challenges
- subsequent behavior and outcome

Later, if the pattern occurs repeatedly, *then* you have something to compare.

## Scale of Evidence

One incident:
